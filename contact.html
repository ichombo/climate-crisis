Get Involved

There are many ways to help keep clothes in the cycle:

  • Volunteer at a donation drive or clothing swap event.
  • Donate gently used clothing instead of throwing it away.
  • Spread the word by sharing what you learn about fast fashion with friends and family.
  • Start a swap at your school, workplace, or neighborhood.
